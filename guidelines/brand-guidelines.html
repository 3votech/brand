Brand guidelines

The 3VOtech logo

The 3 in 3VOtech is a backward E, so the name reads EVO. The logo joins those three letters into one shape.

The Ǝ's spine is also the V's stem. The V's long arm is a check mark that cuts through the O, because that is the job: check in, get the work done, check it off. The Ǝ's bars are cut at the same angle as the check, so they read as speed lines. Time is money for installers, and the logo moves.

The colors are Volt: Volt Blue for the Ǝ, Ultraviolet for the V, Spark Cyan for the O, on a deep navy that is never black.

Which version to use

  • Stacked logo. The default. Use it on a sign-in screen, a slide title, a shirt or a hat, anywhere with room around it.

  • Horizontal logo. For wide, short spaces: website and email headers, letterhead, invoices, banners.

  • Mark only. When there's no room for TECH: the app icon, profile pictures, favicons, anything under 120px wide.

Size and spacing

Give the logo room to breathe. Keep clear space on every side at least the height of one Ǝ bar, marked x below. Text, edges and other graphics stay outside it.

xx

Minimum sizes:

  • Horizontal logo: 120px wide on screen, 1 inch wide in print
  • Stacked logo: 64px wide on screen, 0.6 inch wide in print
  • Mark only: 24px wide on screen, 0.25 inch wide in print

Colors

Each color has a job, and they aren't interchangeable. Use the codes exactly as shown. Tap a code to copy it.

One rule to remember: dark means navy, never black. Our dark grounds are Midnight and Navy Ink.

Do's and don'ts

Always use the logo files as they are. These are the common mistakes.

Color logo on white or a light gray.
Dark color logo on navy.
White logo on a busy or colored ground.
Don't change the logo's colors.
Don't stretch or squash it.
Don't rotate or tilt it.
Don't add shadows, glows, outlines or gradients.
