D · Your ligature

newyour first concept, cleaned up

A shared stem, and the V is a check mark

The Ǝ and the V share one stroke, so the letters are linked together the way a monogram is. The V's long arm reads as a check mark, in an app where technicians check in and mark tasks done. You drew that by instinct. Keep it.

  • Kept: the arm rising above the cap line and crossing the O. That overshoot is what gives it energy.
  • Changed: bars level and equal weight, the middle bar shorter like a real E, every stroke the same thickness, Volt colors in place of pure blue and black.
  • One color: a thin gap where the arm crosses the O keeps the crossing readable.
one risk: the slashed O can read as Ø, or as "zero". The color mostly fixes that.

Chakra Petch lowercase

Only the t has horizontal ends to cut. Spacing opened up by 5.5%.

The rule: a cut goes only where a horizontal stroke ends, exactly like the bars of the Ǝ, and always at 54.6°, parallel to the check. Upright strokes keep their square ends, the same as the V's stem. That is why the H stays plain in every version.

Fonts for "tech"

newthe final mark, twelve wordmarks

Two ways to get speed. An upright face with angled cuts (Chakra Petch, Oxanium, Saira) agrees with the mark, whose stem stands straight up. An italic (Kanit, Exo 2, Archivo) adds speed of its own, but slants about 10–12°, a different angle from the 54.6° cuts. Wide tracked caps spanning the mark's width are the classic racing-team signature line.

B · ƎVO letters

revisedwider V, tighter VO

Spaced by eye, not by ruler

  • V: 22% wider and its strokes about 20% heavier, so it carries the same weight as the Ǝ and the O.
  • Kerning: a V's slanted side leaves a pocket of empty space next to the O, so an equal measured gap looks too wide. The O now sits closer than the Ǝ does, so the two gaps look equal.
  • Overshoot: your eyes are fine 😄 The V's point drops 4 units below the baseline on purpose. A point and a curve look short if they stop exactly on the line.

On gradients and shading

Keep the master flat

The SVG you hand to a printer, a website or a shirt vendor should be flat color. Gradients die in one-color printing, embroidery and small sizes.

Let overlaps be the depth

Mixed colors where the shapes cross read as transparency, so the mark gets dimension without a single gradient stop.

iOS adds the glass for you

Built in Icon Composer with each letter on its own layer, iOS 26 gives the icon Liquid Glass highlights and depth for free. Our assets/expo.icon is still Expo's template icon, so this is a real 0.9 task: the icon is native, it moves the fingerprint.
